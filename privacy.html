Who is responsible

The data controller is Nils Lehmann Podadera (Altuz), NIF/NIE, Postal address. For anything related to your data, write to projectaltuz@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you request admission: your email (required), the name for your card, where and when you run, your preferred number, who sent you, the pieces you’d wear, and a record of your consent (date, time and the exact text you accepted). Our hosting providers also process technical data such as your IP address briefly, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To manage your admission request and the waitlist, and to email you about your request, Drop 001 and the society. Legal basis: your consent (article 6.1.a GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what was done before.
  • To count visits, anonymously and without cookies, only if you allow “Footfall” in the cloakroom. Legal basis: your consent.
  • To show you Altuz on Instagram or TikTok, only if you allow “Invitations” in the cloakroom. Legal basis: your consent.

We don’t sell your data and we don’t make automated decisions about you. Admission is reviewed by people.

How long we keep it

Until you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete it, and at most 24 months after the Drop 001 pre-sale closes. If you become a member, we keep what the membership needs for as long as it lasts.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase, which stores the admission requests in its EU region chosen for the project.
  • Vercel Inc., which hosts the website. Vercel is based in the United States and transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Email provider, once chosen, to send you emails.
  • Meta Platforms and TikTok, only if you allow “Invitations”.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw your consent. Write to projectaltuz@gmail.com and we’ll reply within one month. If you think we haven’t handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Age

Admission requests are only for people aged 14 or over.

Security

Requests are sent over an encrypted connection and stored in a database that the public can write to but not read.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and, if the change matters, we’ll tell you by email.
