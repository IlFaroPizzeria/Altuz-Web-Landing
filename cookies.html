The cloakroom

When you arrive, we hand you a cloakroom ticket. It asks what you want to leave with us. Nothing optional runs until you choose, and you can change your choice whenever you want from Cloakroom settings.

The door (always on)

NameWhat it doesWhereHow long
altuz_consentRemembers your cloakroom choice.Your browser (local storage)12 months
altuz_introRemembers that you’ve already seen the entrance.Your browser (session storage)Until you close the tab

These are needed for the website to work as you asked, so they don’t need consent.

Footfall (optional)

Vercel Web Analytics counts visits and pages in aggregate. It doesn’t use cookies and doesn’t identify you. It only loads if you allow “Footfall”.

Your browser
